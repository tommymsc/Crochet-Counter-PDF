Privacy Policy — Crochet Counter & PDF

LauraApps · Effective November 25, 2026

Data and permissions

The current offline version does not include advertising, analytics, accounts or an internet permission. Projects, row counts and reading settings are stored locally on the device. The Android document picker grants access to PDF files selected by the user; the app does not upload them.

Storage and deletion

Users can remove projects or clear app data using Android settings. Android's system backup may include app data according to device and account settings. Backup services are controlled by the operating system.

Changes

We will update this policy before releasing features that change data collection or sharing, including advertising.

Contact

Contact LauraApps through the developer contact listed on the Google Play store listing.

Política de privacidad

La versión actual funciona sin conexión, sin publicidad, cuentas ni analítica. Los proyectos y ajustes se guardan en el dispositivo. Los PDF seleccionados mediante el selector de Android no se suben a servidores. Puedes borrar los datos desde los ajustes de Android. Las copias de seguridad dependen de la configuración del sistema. Actualizaremos esta política antes de introducir publicidad u otros cambios en el tratamiento de datos. Contacto: datos del desarrollador LauraApps en Google Play.
