Privacy Policy — Crochet Row Counter & PDF

LauraApps · November 25, 2026

Local content

Projects, row counts and reading settings are stored locally. PDF access is granted through the Android document picker; we do not upload your PDFs. Android system backup may include app data according to your device settings. You can delete projects or clear app data.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Google may collect and share IP address (including approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and performance data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and security. The app requests consent where required and provides privacy options when applicable. Advertising requests require network access; core functions work offline. See Google partner data practices and Google Privacy Policy. You may manage advertising identifiers through Android settings.

Contact
